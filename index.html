Why this Project

My goal with Nicole’s guide is to relearn how to romanticize my everyday life experiences like I did when I was a child. It is filled with the ingenuity of the new things I encounter but also from the memories, the company and stories that occur at every place I visit. The inspiration of this guide comes from a few conversations I had with the person I consider my soulmate. If this person is reading this, I hope my guide can be as iconic as Dua Lipa’s one day.

About

How Nicole's Reviews Work

My reviews are more like short stories that can be placed together to form an enchanting mosaic of all my experiences and memories. That is what life is all about, living in the present and being open to the surprises the universe has in store for you. My cherished memories and experiences are all but random selections of who I happened to be with, where we decided to go, who else happened to be there and what ended up happening. As I think heavily on all my previous memories, it saddens me that I cannot remember as many details as I would have liked. There are so many names, places, and faces I have encountered and now forgotten. This guide will be an archive for my future self, so when I get a nostalgic memory I can really look back and reflect on life and be thankful for all the amazing experiences it has gifted me. This guide is not only so I can share my recommendations, but so that my memories can live on. I know many look at nostalgia as a negative feeling, but surprisingly it is one of the feelings I enjoy the most. At any moment, you can experience a scent, a taste, a sound, or a scene that can trigger a memory that transports you into the deepest parts of your subconscious. What a blessing it is to remember something precious that could have been forever forgotten to the past. Getting to relive that old memory for even a moment allows you not only to relive those feelings but also to see the memory now with kinder eyes and with a clearer understanding of how things developed up to this very moment. You have come so far, and that version in your nostalgic memory would be so proud of the person you are today.

This guide will never have negative reviews. I do not believe in tearing people down because of a single negative interaction or experience. We are complex creatures and the world is not always an easy place to live in. Sometimes we have bad days, misunderstandings, and miscommunications with each other. I find that you can usually pinpoint one of those three things as the culprit behind most negative interactions. That is why I refuse to form a deterministic opinion about a place, person or thing. I also do not desire to make life more difficult for those around me, and I do not wish to put more negativity out in the world. If something is truly a negative experience, I simply will not write about it and will make an effort to give it another opportunity in the future. So often it is easier for me to focus on the negative and to give in to disappointment and anxiety, so this guide is my own challenge to focus on the good things that happen and the magical experiences life puts in front of me.

How Nicole’s Star Ratings Work

I am an unreliable narrator who doesn’t necessarily know the end of the story and is only able to share as much as the present allows her to know. For that reason, think about the number of stars a place is given as how much of a specific experience resonates with my soul in that moment. Things that could resonate with my soul are the quality of food, the hospitality and authenticity of a place, and the company surrounding me. I will make an effort to give a detailed explanation in my reviews. I will never take stars away, only add them to a place, just like you cannot delete memories but only build on them. I will do my best to continue to add reviews to those places, but I also will not force stories. As I stated before, I am the most unreliable narrator you could imagine and may not visit a place again. This is not to say that you should not consider that place a place worth visiting to form your own memories. Take my experiences as completely biased because there are so many details my brain may have skipped or missed that would be so incredibly significant for you. My stars are for my own use, and to help me remember my experiences, and force me to try things again and do things in a different way. Life sometimes gets in the way, and I’m only one person who might only get one chance to try a place. In no way should I cloud your own judgment or keep you from forming your own opinions. My goal with this guide is not for you to take my tastes and reviews as something to hold in super high regard. Instead, I hope my stories can help encourage you to try new things, not just in food and drink, but even in how you connect with those around you and form your very own precious memories.
