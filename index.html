Why this project

Every good meal, perfect cup of coffee and unexpected favourite spot ends up scattered across old texts, notes and photos. This guide puts all of it in one place: a map I can keep adding to, with honest reviews of where I've been and a running list of where I want to go next.

If you're visiting one of these cities, I hope it points you somewhere you love too.
